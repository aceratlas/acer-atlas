# Acer Atlas v7.7

- Encyclopedia sort/species controls removed to maximize visible search and browsing space.
- Encyclopedia results always sort alphabetically by canonical cultivar name, with species and ID as stable tie-breakers.
- Search checks display name, Latin/accepted name, synonyms, traits, and history.
- Known duplicate aliases: Acer japonicum ‘Aconitifolium’ is linked to ‘Mai kujaku’ as a synonym per user-provided identification; Esveld’s malformed ‘Adriaan’ record is linked to Mr Maple’s ‘Adrian’s Compact’. Legacy record IDs are retained but hidden from duplicate display.
- No cultivar records physically deleted; duplicate records are hidden in the encyclopedia to avoid breaking saved references.
